About

Pranjal Mendhekar is a UAE-based AI Validation & Risk Governance Leader at a leading financial institution, and formerly Vice President, GenAI/ML Risk at BNY. He brings 8+ years of experience in data science, AI and model risk across major global banks.

His work sits where AI innovation meets accountability: validating models that can't be inspected from the inside, building evaluation frameworks for autonomous agents, and designing governance that lets teams ship AI safely.
