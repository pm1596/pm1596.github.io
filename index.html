Signature talk

Designing the Agentic Evaluation Lifecycle

Metrics, trajectories and guardrails for AI systems that behave differently on every run. Available as a keynote or a half-day workshop.

15,625

possible tool-call sequences for a six-step task with five tools. A 200-case test suite covers just 1.3% of them. This is why agents need a different kind of evaluation.

  1. 01
    The paradigm shiftWhy static test sets and overlap scores stop working for agents.
  2. 02
    Trajectory and outcomeStep-level metrics, sound versus looping paths, and a calibrated judge rubric.
  3. 03
    Structural guardrailsInput, action and output checks, with risk tiers for agent actions.
  4. 04
    The feedback loopTurning failed runs into the next model's training data.

Evaluate the distribution of runs, not the wording of one answer.

Score the path and the outcome separately. Fragile passes hide in between.

Enforce controls in code at the boundary, not in the prompt.

About

Risk practitioner first, speaker second.

Pranjal Mendhekar is a UAE-based AI Validation & Risk Governance Leader at a leading financial institution, and formerly Vice President, GenAI/ML Risk at BNY.

He brings 7+ years across finance and consulting, including data science roles at NICE Actimize and Accenture before leading GenAI and ML risk work in global banking.

His work sits where AI innovation meets accountability: validating models that can't be inspected from the inside, building evaluation frameworks for autonomous agents, and designing governance that lets teams ship AI safely.

He has delivered a recurring talk series with upGrad, joined an international industry panel on AI model risk with leaders from FIS, TCS and SS&C, and speaks at Secure AI Summit UAE 2026 in Dubai.
